Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and the controls you have over it — without the legalese fog.

← Back home About Contact Terms Privacy

1 What we collect

  • Account info — your email (or Google account) via Firebase Authentication.
  • Your account details — name, photo, and phone/WhatsApp, shown to students you message.
  • Listings you publish — a hostel's name, location, price, distance, necessities, description, and photos. All of this is public by design — see section 4.
  • Messages — the conversations you have with other members, so threads can be delivered and displayed.
  • Saved search filters — the price range, distance, and necessities you set on Browse, used to power Recommended and match notifications.
  • Activity — a "last active" timestamp shown as "active recently" to whoever you're chatting with, only if you've enabled it.
  • Reports & feedback — anything you report, or quick feedback you leave on a listing, so our team can review reports and other students can see feedback.
  • Notifications — a device token, only if you opt into browser push notifications.

2 What we don't collect

  • No GPS or precise location tracking — distance and area are only what you type in yourself.
  • No third-party ad trackers, and we don't sell your data to anyone.
  • We don't scan your messages to build an advertising profile of you.

3 How it's used

Your saved filters power Browse's Recommended section and match notifications — weighing price, distance, area, and necessities to surface listings worth a look. Messages and notifications exist to run the chat itself. Reports and feedback are used to keep listings honest and help other students decide. Your theme and accent-color choices are saved only in your own browser's local storage, never on our servers.

4 Nothing about a listing is hidden

Earlier versions of apps like this one sometimes lock extra details behind a private code. HostelHive deliberately does the opposite: price, exact location, necessities, contact info, and every photo on a listing are visible to any signed-in student, immediately, with no unlock step — because all of that is exactly what you need to decide quickly, not something worth gating.

5 Who can see what

  • Every published listing — and its full details — is visible to any signed-in student.
  • Feedback left on a listing is visible to anyone viewing it, not just the owner.
  • Members you block can no longer show you their listings or message you.
  • "Active recently" status is visible to others only if you've turned that setting on.
  • Messages in a thread are visible only to the two people in it. They're transmitted over an encrypted connection and stored in our database — this isn't end-to-end encrypted, so please avoid sending anything you wouldn't want stored.

6 Where it's stored

HostelHive runs on Firebase (Google Cloud) infrastructure. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest using Firebase's own storage encryption.

7 Your controls

  • Edit or delete any listing you've published, any time, from My Listings.
  • Toggle whether "active recently" is visible to others.
  • Block or report a member or listing directly from its detail view.
  • Delete your account entirely, any time, from Settings → Danger zone — this removes your profile and every listing you've published immediately (see the Danger zone for exactly what stays behind: past conversations remain with whoever you messaged, the same way deleting your number doesn't erase a friend's texts).

8 Notifications

If you enable browser push notifications, we use them to alert you to new messages and new listings that match your saved search. You can revoke this permission at any time through your browser's site settings.

9 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date below. Continuing to use HostelHive afterward means you accept the update.
