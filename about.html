About HostelHive

Every hostel near campus, sorted by price, distance, and what actually matters — no site visits wasted.

← Back home About Contact Terms Privacy

⬡ Why we built this

Finding student housing usually means a dozen WhatsApp groups, half-answered DMs, and a walking tour of places that turn out to be nothing like the photo. HostelHive puts every listing in one place instead — price, distance to campus, and the necessities that actually decide whether a room works for you, all visible up front. Nothing about a hostel is locked behind a code or a "message to unlock" gate.

⬡ How it works

Browse filters by price range, distance, and necessities like WiFi, water, security, or meals — set them once and Recommended keeps surfacing new listings that fit, with a notification the moment one goes up. Save the ones worth a second look, message the owner directly to ask questions or arrange a viewing, and leave quick feedback afterward so the next student searching knows what you found.

◎

Nothing hidden

Price, location, necessities, and photos are public on every listing — no unlock codes, no gated details standing between you and a decision.

🛰

Verified & vigilant

Block and Report are one tap away on every listing, and a real person reads every report that comes in.

⚡

Fast by design

Filter, compare, and message an owner in minutes — built for a student who needs a room sorted, not another app to browse endlessly.

⬡

Owned by the lister

Anyone can list a hostel they manage, edit it any time, and see exactly how many students have viewed it.

⬡ Who's behind it

HostelHive is built and looked after by a small, independent team — the kind that reads its own support inbox. If a listing looks off, something breaks, or it could simply work better for students, we want to hear about it.
