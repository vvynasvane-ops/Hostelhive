2 Who can use HostelHive

  • One account per person, under your own identity — no impersonating someone else.
  • Anyone can browse and message owners; anyone can list a hostel they actually manage or own.
  • Everything you publish on a listing — price, location, necessities, photos — should be genuinely accurate.

3 Your account & your listings

You're responsible for what happens under your account, and for keeping every listing you publish accurate and up to date — price changes, a fully booked room, or a necessity that's no longer available should be reflected promptly. Nothing about a listing is hidden or locked behind a code; everything you publish is visible to every student on HostelHive.

4 Community conduct & safety

HostelHive exists to help students find real, available housing quickly. You agree not to:

  • Publish a fake, duplicate, or already-rented listing, or misrepresent its price, location, or condition;
  • Harass, threaten, or deliberately deceive another member;
  • Solicit a deposit or payment before a student has seen the room in person or on a video call;
  • Post content that's sexually explicit, hateful, or promotes violence;
  • Use the app for unrelated advertising, spam, or commercial solicitation;
  • Attempt to access another member's account.

View a room in person, or on a video call, before paying anything, and keep early conversations with an owner on HostelHive. Use Block or Report on any listing or member that makes you uncomfortable — every report is reviewed by a person, not an algorithm.

You keep ownership of whatever you post — your listing photos, descriptions, and messages. By posting it, you give HostelHive permission to store and display it within the app so the service can actually work. We can remove a listing, or restrict an account, that violates these terms.
